# Death Certificates

If you're working with a funeral director or hospice, they'll usually take care of ordering the death certificate. If not, you can order local Department of Health.

Be prepared to answer these questions for the death certificate:

- Full name
- Current address
- Social security number
- Birthdate and place of birth
- Father’s name and place of birth
- Mother’s name and place of birth
- Education
- Marital status and name of their surviving spouse
- Cause of death
- The date the death occurred
- The area the death occurred
- The time the death occurred

You'll want to order multiple copies of death certificates. You may need it for:

- filing the final tax return
- transferring titles
- close out financial accounts (bank, investment, retirement)
- life insurance claim
